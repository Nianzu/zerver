Na2

Na2 is the second robot in the Sodium (Na) lineup. Its improvements on its predecessor include a more modular structure, quick-swap battery, and less complicated electronics, doing away with 5v drive and relying on 3.3v motors. This makes Na2 cheaper, easier to upgrade and maintain, and more useful. However, the drive system was a regression from the previous drive system, causing the vehicle to be slow and weak.

Read more about the Sodium line of mini-robots on the Sodium page!

Na1

Na1 was a project I undertook in a single day. The goal was to design, manufacture, and program as small a robot as possible in a single day. The project was very fun, and I learned a lot about torque and proper motor selection (which I had not done). The robot is able to drive around using a touch joystick on a webpage, and recharges using USB-c. Future versions in the NA line will hopefully have a little more power and speed, and a more reliable drive system.

Read more about the Sodium line of mini-robots on the Sodium page!

Hoverpen

Inspired by the Novium line of hoverpens, this DIY hoverpen is a conversation starter! Using commonly available magnets and the beloved Pilot G2 pen core, this design won't break the bank or your wrist. The design uses 1 (one) 3mm x 2mm magnet, and 38 (thirty eight) 8mm x 2mm magnets. The magnets don't have to be incredibly strong, I purchased a cheap magnet kit off amazon, and have many magnets left over. Assembly is easy, but may require light taps with a hammer to set the magnets correctly. All the magnets are oriented the same way!
